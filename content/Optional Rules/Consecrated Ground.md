Under the curse of Strahd, creatures cannot gain the benefits of a long rest except on **consecrated ground**.

### What is Consecrated Ground?
A consecrated area is one that has been sanctified by ritual or faith, creating a temporary bastion against the draining influence of the mists. This includes:
- Permanent religious sites (e.g., churches, shrines, crypts) while hallowed.
- Areas sanctified using a **consecration kit**.
- Places secured through powerful magical rituals.

### Resting on Consecrated Ground
While on consecrated ground, creatures are immune to the restless influence of Barovia and may complete a long rest normally. If a creature begins a long rest on consecrated ground but leaves before completing it, the benefits of the rest are lost.

### Breaking Consecration
Consecration can be broken if:
- The sanctifying object (such as holy relics or a silver circle) is removed or destroyed.
- The area is defiled by acts of profound evil or Strahd's direct influence.
- A duration expires (as with a consecration kit).

*At the GM’s discretion, other locations—such as the hearth of a truly loyal ally or a fortress secured by the party’s own effort—may also qualify as consecrated ground.*
