## Character Lore

Fought [[Dastdan]] for Strahd, won and was turned into a Vampire Spawn for Strahd. He has no recollection of this happening. Will have visions and urges but doesn't know where they come from. If the player goes along with them the content becomes more violent and harder to resist (dc). If the player resists early on then they are given a sense of urgency rather than a higher dc.

Strahd may command the player directly in rare moments. This uses a similarly scaled Wisdom save, but success does not negate the command entirely. On a success the player resists but suffers a limited setback (charm-like restriction, disadvantage against Strahd). On a failure the command partially takes hold for a short duration, but is never total domination (use PC's reaction for something).

If the player consistently follows the visions and urges, they will be aligned with Strahd’s goals and may fight for him at the end of the campaign unless that influence is broken or rejected.

For this to work, the dreams/visions should not clearly identify Strahd at first. Early visions point to places, people, or artifacts that appear to oppose him. This earns PC trust and party buy-in while the character is unknowingly acting as Strahd’s pawn.
