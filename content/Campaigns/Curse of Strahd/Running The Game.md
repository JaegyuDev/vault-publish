## Less Rest
 We're moving away from the more NPC drama into a crawl. What this means for you is that long rests are not guaranteed. The only places you're guaranteed a safe long rest is in an Inn and in a house you "own". These can be empty buildings, houses owned by NPCs you've helped, things you've worked for.

### Mechanically for me
* No more long rests outside of a place I don't deem safe.
	* They will be forced into combat for this, and given the benefits of a short rest, as per the Long Rest rules on 370.
* Short rests will trigger a random encounter.
	* Page 373 of the 2024 PHB states: "**Interrupting the rest.** A short Rest is stopped by rolling initiative, casting a spell other than a cantrip, or taking damage"
