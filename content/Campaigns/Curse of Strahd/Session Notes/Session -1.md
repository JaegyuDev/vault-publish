I don't really remember how many sessions we've ran up to this point-- maybe 2-3? This is a recap of all of the things that have happened so far.

The players have completed the Death House adventure, collected some loot and capes, but missed the Strahd related adventure hints.

From there our travelers stepped into the Village of Barovia proper. The party cleric convinced the Father to convert to his religion, and promised to heal his son if he aids the party on their adventure. From there the party also managed to pick up Ireena (?) and Ismark. The party split while the cleric picked up some poison ingredients and the warlock and rouge talked to the crying woman. They had a partial March of the Dead encounter, and then went off towards the Vestani encampment.

During their travel they met a Magic Mushroom Shop, and the owner's name was Merp, and bought mushrooms that roll on the wild magic table when ingested. The cleric bought 10 of them.

After that they continued on the path and ran into Strahd and two werewolves. They were able to beat this encounter by feeding a Magic Mushroom to Strahd while he was grappled. It turned him into a sheep and they tied him up and ran off ahead.
