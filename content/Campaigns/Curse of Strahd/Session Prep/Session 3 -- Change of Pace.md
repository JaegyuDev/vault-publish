# Campaign Pivot: Barovian Survival Horror

## Core Philosophy
Shift from narrative crawl to **resource-draining survival horror**. The party must manage a dwindling pool of hit points, hit dice, and spell slots while seeking rare Safe Spaces. Time = danger; every hour spent traveling risks encounters.

---

## Key Mechanics

### 1. The Safe Space Rule
- **No long rests** except in designated Safe Spaces.
- **Short rests** allowed anywhere (but risky).
- **Safe Space Definition:** Three-tier system:

| Tier | Type | Requirements/Conditions | Example Locations |
|------|------|------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Consecrated Ground** | Conditionally active; often quest-locked | • Vallaki's Church (only while Bones are present)<br>• Abbey of St. Markovia (psychologically unsafe)<br>• Krezk's Shrine (may require ritual) |
| 2 | **Earned Hearth** | Granted by loyal ally; revocable if standing changes | • Mirabel's house (if daughter saved)<br>• Blue Water Inn (if Martikovs befriended)<br>• Van Richten's Tower<br>• Vistani camp (if truce sworn) |
| 3 | **Consecration Kit** | Consumable; creates temporary, beacon-like sanctuary | • Ritual takes 1 hour, lasts 8 hours<br>• Glows visibly, attracting encounters<br>• Defendable "dungeon rest" option |

**Introduce via NPC/lore:** *"The mists drain those who sleep under the open sky. You need a hearth lit with welcome, ground hallowed by faith, or a place made your own through blood, sweat and tears to truly rest."*

### 2. Travel & Time
- **Point crawl system:** Travel between locations costs time.
- **Danger Die:** Roll d6 per travel segment; on 1, encounter. Escalate to d4 in dangerous areas.
- **Time tracking is public:** Use tally marks. "It's been 4 hours since your last short rest. Dusk is approaching."

### 3. Max HP Reduction
- Used by Night Hags, Wights, Vampire Spawn, etc.
- **Cannot be restored by hit dice or short rests.**
- **Only long rest in a Safe Space restores max HP.**
- **Narrative cue:** *"You look down at your wound. Where there should be blood you instead find your flesh dying. Your max health is permanently limited until your next long rest."*

## Encounter Sequence: Old Bonegrinder to Vallaki

### Encounter 0: Rules & Setup
- Announce Safe Space rule before travel.
- Establish point-crawl/time tracking.

### Encounter 1: Old Bonegrinder (Curated Horror)
**Goal:** Drain resources, deliver monologue, establish hags as corrupting philosophers.
- **Phase 1 (The Bite):** Hags ambush, spread damage, use Life Drain on different PCs, control battlefield (Sleep, Eyebite, Phantasmal Killer). Drop one PC to 0 for drama but stabilize.
- **Phase 2 (The Release):** After 3–4 rounds or when party is bloodied, Morgantha signals retreat.
- **Phase 3 (The Monologuette):** Morgantha goes Ethereal, delivers speech:
  > *"_“Every young bird must try its wings. The first flight is the only truth. Strong wings soar. Weak wings… fail. They fall. The forest floor is littered with those who tried to fly before they were ready. That is the natural order. No amount of wishing changes the strength of a wing. Leave my house now before one of you is left scattered."*
  > Could even be a bluff. If the players call it let them kill the night hags.
  > If they are killed then the stones behind the house can be cleaned and will act as consecrated land later.

### Encounters 2–5: The Road to Vallaki
**Overarching Threat:** The "little bird" prophecy.

1.  **Combat 1 (The Drain):** 5–6 Wolves + 1 Dire Wolf. Goal: harry, wound, force resource use. They retreat if seriously injured.
2.  **Social/Horror (The Girl):** **Wren** ("little crow"), 9 y/o, lost. She knows the way to Vallaki and her mother's house (blue lantern). She offers Safe Space as reward. Slow (20 ft.), frail (4 HP), hurts group Stealth.
3.  **Combat 2 (The Toll):** 2–3 Vampire Spawn. Obsessed with Ireena (if present) or the party's "new blood." Relentless.
4.  **Combat 3 (Consequence – if slowed by Wren):** **The Hearse:** Wight + 3–4 Zombies. Wight's Life Drain synergizes with max HP damage from hags.
	* if the party isn't doing too well now, this encounter can be tuned back by not casting life drain.

## Vallaki Arrival & Consequences

### If Wren Survives:
- **At Gate:** Guards recognize her, usher party in with respect.
- **Mirabel's House (Blue Lantern):** Emotional reunion. Mirabel: *"My little crow! You flew home!"* Grants Safe Space. *"This hearth is yours. You can rest here."* Becomes social ally.

### If Wren Dies or is Lost:
- **At Gate:** Hostile guards, demand bribe/confiscation.
- **Mirabel's House:** She asks, *"Have you seen a girl? My little crow has been missing since early this morning..."*
	- If the party confesses she died, Mirabel still let's them stay with her.
	- If the party does not she will ask them to find her. This does not open her house to them.
### If Wren is Abandoned:
-  Mirabel will ask them to find Wren.
- Party must find other Safe Space (Inn, etc.).

## Vallaki's Church Crisis

**Goal:** Use the party's need for rest to drive the "Bones of St. Andral" quest.

### If Party Has No Safe Space:
- **Blue Water Inn** offers stable (coin required), but it's **not a Safe Space**.
- **Father Lucian** is nervous; church feels "inert" (Religion/Insight check reveals loss of consecration).

### Forced Revelation (Vampire Spawn Attack):
- At dusk, 2–3 vampire spawn **brazenly attack the church** during evening service.
	- If they take the stables do it then.
- Townsfolk cry: *"To the church! The holy ground will hold them!"*
- Spawn **breach the doors**—revealing sanctuary is broken.
- After fight, spawn flee if wounded.
- **Key NPC accuses party:** *"They got in! Why did they get in *this* time, when *you* are here?"*
- **Father Lucian confesses:** The bones are gone.
- **Lore drop:** *"If St. Andral's won't protect us, nowhere in this town is truly safe for a full night's sleep."*

**Result:** The party must **restore the bones** to create a reliable Safe Space in Vallaki.

## DM Notes & Tips

### Pacing
- Narrate the drain: *"The mist clings to you, making short rests uneasy. Your muscles don't fully recover."*
- Track time publicly.
- Use morale: Many Barovian creatures flee or negotiate if losing.

### Hags as Corruptors
- Their monologue is **philosophical poison**, not just foreshadowing.
- Let Wren's behavior (tremors, nightmares, mentions of "the nice man in my dreams") sow doubt about the "weak bird."

### Consecration Kits (If Used)
- Contents: holy water, sanctified incense, silver dust.
- Ritual: 1 hour, Religion proficiency helpful.
- Effect: Creates 30-ft. Safe Space for 8 hours. **Glows, attracting encounters.** Must be defended.

### Adjusting Difficulty
- If party is crushed, Vistani can offer "safe passage" to camp for a future favor—maintains "no long rest" but gives merciful out.

### Thematic Throughline
- **Chapter 1 (Road):** Physical drain & moral choice (Wren).
- **Chapter 2 (Vallaki):** Social crisis & restoration of sanctuary (Bones).
- The land tests them physically, then morally, then socially.
