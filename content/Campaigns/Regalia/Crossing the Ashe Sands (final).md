>? Session Goal: Locate a missing caravan, uncovering secrets best left forgotten.

You will need the rules on \[SoD 2:Traversing the wastes 51]

### Setup // Encounter one

> [!coldopen] You are a group of mercenaries. Paid to keep it off the Senate books, and paid even more to forget doing it. Your handler--your only contact--is Xarxies, and you’ve done operations for him before, though the secrecy is always exhausting. Such is life in the Imperium Regalis, as your senate contact insists. Speak those words outside the walls of this city, however, and few would understand, and even fewer would take you seriously, for trust in the capitol dwindles across the Regalian Empire.

> [!quote] You're gathered in a room, tucked away in the back of this ran down inn. The chairs wobble when you shift your weight. The table has names, numbers, and crude art scratched into the top. The room smells damp. Across from you is a man, an almost uncanny kind of pale. Thin, but healthy. Here's noticeably clean. More than what you can say about most people here.

#### Roleplaying Xarxies
* Kind of stuck up.
* Secretive. Only tells the party what they need to know, and even that's a stretch.
* Tells the party the following:
  1. There is a missing caravan that was accompanied by a military unit.
  2. They (the Senate) stopped receiving messages from the unit 3 days ago.
  3. The caravan was carrying a shipment for the Senate from a small town deep in the Ashe Sands back.
  4. The Ashe Sands are dangerous. Not just because of what monsters lurk there, but also the heat during the day, and the freezing temps at night.
  5. Any supplies they need will be covered for them. \[hands them a credit with the wax seal of Regalia].

The party can take the time now to find different things they'd need before adventuring out into the sands. The player's might want water, some thick travel bedding, a heat source, and thin clothes. They'll have to plan on weather they're going to travel at night or day. **Communicate this.**

### Travel Through the Ashe Sands
> [!quote] The Ashe Sands are unforgiving. Many die out here long before they ever meet whatever kind of life could manage surviving in this place. The sands ahead of you stretch on without end. What should be pale and shifting is instead dark and dull. When you rub the sand between your fingers, it leaves an ashy residue- coarse, dry, and burns your finger tips.

Have the players roll a DC 15 History check.

> [!quote] These sands have been here so long that most believe they were formed with the world itself. Others tell stories passed from one generation to the next until they’ve become little more than wives’ tales: a clash of titans, a god’s wrath against an ancient civilization, or even a meteor from the “space in between” scarring the land. Whatever the truth, all but the most stubborn life refuses to grow here now.


> [!optional] This can be cuttable depending on how quickly the players are progressing through the content
