* Language Structure  
    * Prefix denotes place type  
        * Vil = small settlement
        * Rhal
    * Independent roots exist in clipped form  
        * Example: Shuun'd (pronounced shoon-d)  
            * Likely means thin current / stream  
            * Terminal apostrophe marks reduced or dropped ending  
    * Bound form used when attached to prefix  
        * Shuun'd -> Shuunde (pronounced shoond-eh)  
            * Added -e acts as locative or construct marker  
            * Smooths phonetic transition in compounds  
    * Structural rule  
        * Independent roots end in hard stop or apostrophe  
        * Bound forms add -e or -a  
        * Prefixes require bound forms  

* Vil'Shuunde  
    * Vil = settlement classification  
    * Shuunde = bound form of Shuun'd  
    * Meaning: Settlement of the Stream  
    * Predates the Sand  
        * Originally accompanied a small stream  
        * Softer phonology reflects older, water-based lexicon
